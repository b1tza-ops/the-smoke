House rules

0.2% house edge under basic strategy
  • Dealer stands on every 17, soft ones included.
  • Blackjack pays 3:2.
  • Double on any two cards, including after a split.
  • Split up to four hands. Split aces take one card each.
  • Insurance offered on a dealer ace, paying 2:1.
  • Late surrender before any other action, forfeiting half.
  • A fresh shoe every hand, so counting cards gets you nowhere.

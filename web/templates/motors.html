Worth knowing before you sign

  • The Underground does half the bus journey and does not run to Hackney. Anything from the Marlow Estate up beats it, everywhere.
  • Being wanted is what makes driving a decision. A patrol stops you before you set off — you lose the journey and {{ pulled_over_minutes }} minutes to a cell, though never the petrol. The chance is your heat against how much the car shows off, so a bicycle is never stopped and a Sable GT at full heat is a coin toss.
  • Your garage holds what your address allows: nothing below a van, one up to a modern house, three in a penthouse.
  • The forecourt buys back at half what you paid. A car is not a savings account.

The Smoke is a free text-based crime RPG set in London. You start with nothing in Camden — no money worth the name and no reputation at all — and work your way up through five districts of a city that keeps moving while you are away.

What you do

Five districts, and no shortcuts

  • Crime — ten jobs across Camden, Brixton, Soho, Shoreditch and Hackney, from shoplifting to a canal handover. Every one can put you in a cell.
  • Training — fourteen gyms and four stats, with weight classes that decide what you can realistically fight.
  • Fighting — opponents in every district, plus rated player-versus-player with streaks and a leaderboard.
  • Money — a bank, a global item market, a black-market fence, a loan shark in Soho, and a casino in Golden Square with the odds published.
  • Property — eight homes from a tent to a penthouse, each with rent, storage and recovery to match.

Nothing here needs an app, a download or a payment. It runs in a browser, it works without JavaScript, and your progress carries on whether you are logged in or not.
